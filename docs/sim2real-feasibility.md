# Can we learn RF maps without buying tower data? Feasibility note, September 27, 2026

**Answer:** A no-purchase research path exists, but a credible worldwide signal-strength predictor does **not** follow automatically from public terrain and a cell-position map. Use a physics simulator to generate synthetic supervision; calibrate sector and material parameters where real measurements exist; test transfer on whole held-out regions. Where target-region measurements are absent, label output `unvalidated_transfer`. Do not publish simulated signal as observed RF or guaranteed service.

## Small, falsifiable first test

1. **Rights and inputs.** Keep DoNext's CC BY 4.0 measurements and their exact sampled source rows, OSM/Geofabrik ODbL geometries, ESA WorldCover CC BY 4.0 classes and Copernicus DEM attribution/version in a source ledger. Use a bounded Dortmund scene. Do not synthesize tower positions from OpenCellID without confirming identity and position quality. For initial controlled trials, start with a few explicitly assumed or independently surveyed transmitter positions, or simulate a range of hypotheses. No assumed site is presented as real.
2. **Baselines first.** Compare (a) per-cell sector/distance regression, (b) SPLAT! terrain/antenna baseline and (c) Sionna-generated features plus supervised fine-tuning. All receive the same *permitted* training observations and identical predefined test splits. Include a synthetic-only ablation and an observed-only baseline. Never train anchors, material values, or hyperparameters on the test route/region.
3. **Simulation domain randomization.** Vary uncertain heights, azimuth/tilt, EIRP, bands, patterns, material permittivity, building heights and position errors with clearly stated priors. Use synthetic scenes as augmentation, not as independent evidence of actual site metadata. Fine-tune on measured regions. If UK Ofcom is used, verify exact dataset rights, serving-cell identity and metric compatibility first; road measurements may not include enough metadata for per-sector alignment.
4. **Evaluation gate.** Hold out whole route segments **and whole unseen areas**. Report MAE and signed bias in dB, per-region/per-band errors, spatial residual maps, uncertainty calibration, abstention coverage and comparison to the same baseline. Vary simulated priors and test sensitivity. A pooled improvement on one corridor without held-out-region gain is not a transfer success. The current physical-context experiment had 6.77 dB spatial-CV MAE versus 6.51 dB sector baseline, despite one better route holdout; see [its results](./clutter-research.md). Reuse that failure as the baseline to beat, not a reason to invent a coverage surface.
5. **Ship with a grade.** Only validated observed regions can show `model_prediction` with exact validation scope. A region with no local measured RF, even if a simulated map looks plausible, remains `unvalidated_transfer` and needs conspicuous explanation of the assumptions and missing local error estimate. No isotropic coverage circles or service claims.

## Is reinforcement learning useful?

Predicting a fixed location's RF power from scene and transmitter parameters is primarily **supervised regression, inverse parameter estimation and physics-guided sim-to-real/domain adaptation**, not an RL problem. There is no natural sequential action/reward in the static map prediction itself. RL or a simpler active-learning/bandit policy could later choose where a consenting surveyor should measure next, or adapt simulator experiment selection, using a defined reward such as held-out error reduction and respecting travel/privacy costs. That is a separate, later experiment. Do not call an RL agent a substitute for real RF labels or antenna metadata.

## Immediate product boundary

No physical simulator, latent tower solver, CellMapper import or global RF model has been built in this repository. The only live sector-path layer is a route-bound diagnostic, and the Israel inventory holds estimated cell locations **without measured signal strength**. A research prototype may start without purchased data, but scale, site identity, geographic bias and rights remain real constraints. The practical strategic source is privacy-conscious, opt-in crowdsourced measurements with explicit reuse terms and retained serving-cell identity where devices expose it.
