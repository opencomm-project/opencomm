# Sector and physical-context experiment, September 27, 2026

This is a reproducible **research experiment**, not a coverage map or a claim of tower location. The local code is [`scripts/build_clutter_prototype.py`](../scripts/build_clutter_prototype.py); its result is [`assets/clutter-metrics.json`](./assets/clutter-metrics.json). A separate Dortmund map diagnostic can show paths between *estimated reception centers* and observed route points, but such a path is neither a ray traced through a building nor a predicted coverage footprint. No off-route signal validation exists.

## Modeling and evaluation

The model uses 9,455 eligible sampled code-C NR readings from cell groups with at least 20 readings. In each validation split, a signal-weighted reception-center anchor is estimated from **training readings only**. Matching keys use anonymized code, `cell_index`, PCI and EARFCN, not full CGI. PCI can repeat. It discards paths beyond 750m from that noisy anchor. A baseline LightGBM uses log distance, bearing harmonics, and east/north offset. The enrichment adds OSM footprint intersection count along the straight anchor-to-receiver line, WorldCover tree/urban fractions, and DEM surface height change/max excess. It cannot prove an actual RF path or building blockage. Both models use identical fitting settings and split membership. Five contiguous longitude blocks on training rows are compared; a middle-longitude 20% route segment is held out from the CV and tested once. The held-out readings are all on the *same* route and may remain autocorrelated.

| Test | Sector/distance baseline MAE | With physical-context features MAE | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Weighted 5-fold geographic CV, 5,098 eligible validation readings | 6.51 dB | **6.77 dB** | Enrichment worse overall; improved 3 of 5 folds, with uneven and partly non-overlapping eligible anchor coverage. |
| Untouched contiguous route segment, 1,869 eligible readings | 4.92 dB | 4.31 dB | Modest local improvement, insufficient to override mixed CV or establish out-of-route skill. |

No tower-position error metric, off-route test, uncertainty calibration, independent drive survey or public service-quality guarantee is available. Do not advertise a coverage surface based on this experiment. Next meaningful milestone is independently located and matchable sector/site anchors plus observations off the rail route, then time/spatially held-out testing.
